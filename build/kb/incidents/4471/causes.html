5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

parser is the shared date parser. It reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s dates come out flipped: the month and the day trade places.

hint is the booking-v3 form. The hint under the field says MM/DD/YYYY, so people type the month first. The form then reads that text the tenant’s way, day first. Same symptom as parser, different code.

shift is not a flip. The date moves by one day: Tuesday shows as Monday, a meeting lands a day apart in Lisbon and Toronto, only June is off. That is time-zone or daylight-saving conversion. The desk reads these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and counts them where they were filed, under parser: the note under the table. The parser fix does not fix them.

#4471 closed on 5 October. parser is fixed (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales). hint is won't fix: the booking-v3 form is retired. shift has no fix yet.
