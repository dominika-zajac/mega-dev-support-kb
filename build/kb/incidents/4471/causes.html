hint is the booking-v3 form. The hint under the field says MM/DD/YYYY, so people type the month first. The form then reads that text the tenant’s way, day first. Same symptom as parser, different code.

shift is not a flip. The date moves by one day: Tuesday shows as Monday, a meeting lands a day apart in Lisbon and Toronto, only June is off. That is time-zone or daylight-saving conversion. The desk had read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but left them in the count of their tenant’s cause.

One fix does not close the others. The queued parser fix leaves the hint and the shift in place.
